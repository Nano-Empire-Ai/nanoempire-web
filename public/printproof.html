PrintProof is an automated forensic check for self-publishers' interior PDFs — the layout errors that survive your visual check and die on press, caught with page numbers before KDP, IngramSpark, or your print shop ever sees the file.

The 7 pre-press killers

1. Pagination parity

One-page spreads landing on even pages flip the left/right order of every spread after them. In one real 83-page cookbook, a single parity break scrambled 18 spreads.

2. Blank-page runs

Three or more consecutive blank pages read as a production error, and skipped page numbers get files rejected outright.

3. TOC page-count drift

If your table of contents was generated from an earlier pagination pass, every page reference after the drift is wrong — and no upload checker flags it.

4. Trailing separators

A leftover separator at the end of a chapter file can render as a full printed page of nothing but dots.

5. Placeholder and QC leakage

"Lorem", "TK", "TODO", draft notes, and internal QC blocks hiding after your last chapter. One nearly printed inside a $404.82 keepsake book.

6. The unrendered dedication

Front matter that's commented out in your source never makes it to the PDF. If you don't check the final file, you find out from a reader.

7. Font embedding

One unembedded font and the file that looked perfect on your machine breaks on theirs. The most common silent killer of all.
