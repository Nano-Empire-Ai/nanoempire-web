What BidShield Does

BidShield produces scope-honest, source-cited preliminary risk previews from public or non-sensitive business documents. The output identifies:

  • Buried deadlines and addendum conflicts
  • Mandatory forms and missing referenced schedules
  • Insurance and WSIB compliance traps
  • Clarification-worthy ambiguities
  • Document gaps and external dependencies

What BidShield Does Not Do

  • It does not issue compliance rulings
  • It does not replace legal, insurance, or procurement counsel
  • It does not process confidential, privileged, or regulated documents in public preview
  • It does not guarantee bid success or submission compliance

How It Works

1. You share a public tender, RFP, or non-sensitive compliance packet.

2. The kernel extracts mandatory requirements, deadlines, and referenced documents.

3. A human reviewer verifies all Fatal and High findings against source documents.

4. You receive a scope-honest report with clear next actions, missing documents, and explicit limits.

Privacy and Data Handling

Pilot intake accepts only public or non-sensitive business documents. Do not submit medical records, patient identifiers, confidential client pricing, privileged legal documents, credentials, or government-protected material.

Full-package reviews are performed with human verification and a confirmed legal and data-handling scope. Retention, deletion, and processing location are disclosed in the pilot engagement letter.
